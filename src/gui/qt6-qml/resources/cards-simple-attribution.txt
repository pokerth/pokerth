The card artwork in resources/cards-simple/ (52 files named <rank><suit>, e.g.
10c.svg, 12s.svg) comes from the responsive SVG deck

    https://github.com/younestouati/playing-cards-standard-deck

a fork of https://github.com/digitaldesignlabs/responsive-playing-cards. Taken
over is that deck's simplified small-size layout (the "mini-card" that those
cards switch to below 75 px: one large rank glyph plus one suit symbol),
refitted onto the 120x168 card of the PokerTH QML client. The artwork of the
upstream deck is based on Chris Aguilar's "Vectorized Playing Cards" (LGPL 3.0),
with the ace of spades from Byron Knoll's public domain deck.

The same 52 files are shipped as the bundled card deck
data/gfx/qml/cards/default/ (named by engine index, 0.svg..51.svg) and, in a
recoloured four-colour variant, as data/gfx/qml/cards/default4c/. Several other
bundled decks reuse the suit symbols and rank glyphs from there.
